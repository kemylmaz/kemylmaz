### Projects

**🍵 Brewbook** — *Cozy café management sim · Unity · in development*
Players uncover who the twenty recipes hidden in an old recipe book once belonged to, upgrading the shop chapter by chapter until the book is complete. Solo development: game design, core systems, and art direction.

**🚨 [Yanındayım](https://github.com/kemylmaz/yanindayim)** — *Offline-first disaster response app · Flutter*
Built for the first 72 hours after a major earthquake, when base stations are down. Bluetooth LE peer-to-peer distress beacons, an on-device AI assistant for first aid, offline maps with AFAD assembly points, and an always-on SOS screen that bypasses the lock screen so responders can read a victim's health card. Fully on-device — no server, no signal, no internet. I worked on UI/UX and the AI triage system.

**❤️ LovHealth** — *Health-tech mobile app · 🥉 3rd place, AppJam Hackathon*
Detects signs of a heart attack through phone sensors and smartwatch data, then instantly alerts the nearest registered responders and emergency services. I contributed ideation, prototyping, and the pitch.

**⚙️ [OptiForge](https://github.com/kemylmaz/OptiForge-Final)** — *AI-supported generative design platform · concept*
Authored the project document and executive summary for an AI-driven platform optimizing automotive components, defining the problem space and a roadmap for bringing generative design into manufacturing workflows.
